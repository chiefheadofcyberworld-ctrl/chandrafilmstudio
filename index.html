Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services.

What photography services do you offer?

We offer Wedding Photography, Wedding Cinematography, Pre-Wedding Shoots, Birthday & Event Photography, Portrait Photography, and Album Designing. Visit our Services page to explore more details.

Do you cover weddings and pre-wedding shoots?

Yes, we provide wedding photography, cinematic wedding films, and pre-wedding shoots. You can share your specific requirements with us to discuss your project.

Do you travel outside Bihar for projects?

Yes, we provide photography and cinematography services across India. Travel arrangements and additional travel charges may apply depending on the event location. Please share your event date and location to discuss availability and travel costs.

How can I book your photography services?

You can contact us through WhatsApp, phone, or our Contact page. Please share your event date, location, and required services. We will then discuss availability and the booking process.

How much do your photography packages cost?

Our pricing depends on the event type, coverage requirements, and selected services. You can explore our Pricing page or contact us directly for a customised quotation.

How early should I book?

We recommend contacting us as early as possible, especially during the wedding season. Early booking helps us check availability for your preferred event date.

How will I receive my photos and videos?

The delivery method and timeline will be discussed during the booking process. You can let us know whether you require photographs, edited videos, cinematic films, or albums.

Do you provide cinematic wedding films?

Yes, Wedding Cinematography is one of our services. You can discuss your specific film requirements with us before confirming your booking.

Can I customise my photography package?

Yes, you can share your event details, preferred services, and specific requirements with us. We will discuss the available package options based on your needs.
