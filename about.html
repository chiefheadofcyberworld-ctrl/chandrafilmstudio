About Chandra Film Studio

The Studio

Capturing moments,
creating memories.

Welcome to Chandra Film Studio. We are a passionate team of photographers and cinematographers dedicated to documenting life's most precious moments.

Based in Dinara, Rohtas, Bihar, we photograph and film weddings, pre-wedding shoots, birthdays, events and portraits for families in Rohtas and nearby areas.

Our approach is simple — we blend into your celebration, observe quietly, and capture the raw, unscripted emotions that make your story unique. We believe that the best photographs are the ones where you forget the camera is even there.
